# 🌶️ Mexican Seasoning Mix   
# ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️  
- 1 tsp chili powder  
- 1 tsp garlic powder  
- 1 tsp onion powder  
- 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper flakes  
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano   
- 2 tsp paprika  
- 1 1/2 tsp cumin  
- 1/2 tsp salt  
- 1/2 tsp black pepper  
- pinch cinnamon   
- pinch ground clove  
  
Mix together ingredients thoroughly in a bowl and store in a cool place in an airtight container.
