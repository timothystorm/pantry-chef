1. Preheat oven to 400°F   
2. Combine the almond flour and all the ingredients under the spice mix together   
3. In a small bowl beat the one egg. Add your now seasoned flour to a plate and place bowl and plate side by side   
4. Dip the chicken tender in the egg the place it in the plate. Pat the flour on the chicken tender evenly on both sides then place the tender on a baking rack. Repeat until all the tenders are done.   
5. Place in oven for 15 minutes then flip and bake for a remaing 10 minutes. Allow to cool a bit when you first remove them because almond flour is very fragile while piping hot.   
6. Use the same cooking method and times if using an air fryer but you may need to add parchment paper if the plate is prone to sticking
