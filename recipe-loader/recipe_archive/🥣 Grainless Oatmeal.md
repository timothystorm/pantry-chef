# 🥣 Grainless Oatmeal  
⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️  
⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️  
⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️  
- 1 1/2 cup coconut milk, nut milk, or water  
- 6 tbsp coconut flour  
- 2 tbsp ground flax seeds  
- 1 cup egg whites  
  
1. In a small saucepan, bring milk or water to boil.  
2. Turn down to medium heat, then whisk in coconut flour and ground flax seeds. Once water is fully absorbed and the oatmeal is a ‘thick’ consistency, turn heat to low.  
3. Whisk in egg whites until fluffy, then heat for another 1-2 minutes to allow oatmeal to thicken.
