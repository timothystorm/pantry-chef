## 🥣 Peruvian Chicken Soup   
## ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️  
* 1.5 pounds of organic, pasture-raised chicken   
* 48 oz of chicken broth  
* Juice from 2 limes, 1 lemon  
* 1 jalapeño pepper, minced (can omit if you don’t do well with spicy; I don’t like things too spicy and I was fine with this small amount)  
* 3 large carrots, sliced  
* 1 bunch of celery, sliced  
* 1 cup of cilantro, chopped  
* 1 tablespoon of sea salt (half to coat the chicken, half to put in the broth)  
* 1/2 tablespoon of black pepper (half to coat the chicken, half to put in the broth)  
* 1 teaspoon of cumin  
* 1 cup jasmine rice (can omit if sensitive to rice or grains; most people do well with jasmine rice because it is less allergenic and digests higher up in the small intestine, meaning that bacteria causing SIBO don’t get a chance to feed off it and cause digestive distress)  
  
1. Cube the chicken and season with the sea salt and pepper.  
2. Brown the chicken in olive oil or butter/ghee in a pan until it’s just cooked on the outside.  
3. Line a large pot (we used a 6 quart pot) with olive oil or butter/ghee. Add the veggies (jalapeño, celery, carrots), cilantro, sea salt, pepper, and cumin. Let cook on medium for about 5 minutes.  
4. Add the chicken to the pot with the veggies and add the broth and lemon/lime juice.  
5. Bring to a boil and then cover and simmer for 30 minutes.   
6. Cook and store the jasmine rice separately (cooking it in the soup will absorb the liquid. See instructions below, and then mix into the soup when that has finished cooking).
