# Leek & Potato Soup (?)  
- 4 tbsp. butter  
- 1 onion Chopped  
- 3 leeks, sliced  
- 2 potatoes, 2cm cubes  
- 3 1/2 cup vegetable stock  
- 2/3 light cream  
- 2 tbsp. chives  
  
1. melt the butter in a large saucepan over medium heat, add the onion, leeks, and potatoes and sauté for 2-3 minutes, until soft but not brown  
2. pour in the stock and bring to a boil  
3. reduce the heat and simmer for 15 minutes   
4. transfer to food processor or blender and process until smooth  
5. ladle into bowels adding salt and pepper to taste  
6. swirl with the cream and garnish with chives
