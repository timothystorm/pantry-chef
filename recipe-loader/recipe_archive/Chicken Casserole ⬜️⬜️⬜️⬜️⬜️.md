# Chicken Casserole ⬜️⬜️⬜️⬜️⬜️  
- 4 cups broccoli florets   
- 1 bunch scallions, chopped   
- 2 tablespoons olive oil   
- Salt and black pepper to the taste   
- Eggplant  
- 3 cups chicken, cooked and shredded   
- 1 cup chicken stock   
- ½ teaspoon ground nutmeg   
- 2 eggs  
  
Heat up a pan with the oil over medium heat, add onions, mushrooms, salt and pepper, stir, cook for 10 minutes. Pour into a baking dish and top with the chicken and the broccoli. In a bowl, mix stock, eggs, nutmeg, salt and pepper. Stir well, spread over the chicken mix and cook at 350 degrees F for 40 minutes.
