# Chicken Fried Rice (?)  
- 2 cups jasmine rice   
- 3 1/2 cups water  
- 4 medium carrots peeled   
- 4 celery stalks   
- 1 yellow onion  
- cramini mushrooms  
- 3 cloves of garlic  
- 2-3 chicken breasts  
- high smoke point cooking oil  
- 2 Tbsp. sherry or rice wine  
- 2 Tbsp. honey  
- 6 Tbsp. soy sauce or coconut amino   
- 5 eggs quickly whisked together  
- 1/4 green onions  
  
1. Add water to rice in a sauce pan and turn to high heat until rolling boil then turn heat to low, put a lid on and cook for about 15 minutes  
2. While rice is cooking small rice carrots, celery, onion, and mushrooms  
3. Mince garlic and add to diced vegetables   
4. Thin slice chicken breasts  
5. Add about3 Tbsp. cooking oil to large pan or wok set on high heat  
6. The instant the oil starts to smoke add the chicken stir frying until chicken is cooked  
7. Add sherry, 2 Tbsp. soy sauce and honey  
8. Cook chicken for another minute mixing the sauce then set it to the side  
9. Add 2 Tbsp. cooking oil   
10. The instant it starts to smoke add the vegetables and garlic stir frying for about 2 minutes until the vegetables are cooked but still a bit crunchy   
11. Poor the whisked egg over the vegetables and cook omelette style  
12. Once the egg begins to setup stir them in and add the chicken and rice  
13. Add 4 Tbsp. soy sauce and 1 cup peas and green onions  
14. mix it completely together  
15. Add salt and pepper to taste  
16. serve and enjoy
