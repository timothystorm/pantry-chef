# Beef Stock  
- 6 Lbs. Beef soup bones  
- 1 Large onion  
- 3 Large Carrots  
- 1/2 Cup water  
- 2 Celery stalks  
- 1 Large tomato   
- 1/2 Cup chopped parsnip  
- 1 medium potato  
- 4 Sprigs parsley  
- 1 Bay leaf  
- 1 Tablespoon salt  
- 2 teaspoons dried thyme  
- 2 Garlic cloves  
- 12 Cups water  
  
1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees  
2. Quarter the onion.  Scub carrots and chop into 1 inch chunks.  in a large shallow roasting pan add soup bones onion and carrots.  Bake uncovered for 30 minutes, turning occasionally until bones are well browned.  
3. Drain off fat.  Place the bones onion and carrots in a large pot.  Pour 1/2 cup of water into roasting pan to rince and pour liquid into pot.  
4. Scub potato and cut into chunks.  Chop celery into thirds.  Add remaining ingredients to pot.  
5. Bring mixture to a boil then reduce the heat. Cover and simmer for 5 hours.  
6. Drain stock and discard ingredients.
