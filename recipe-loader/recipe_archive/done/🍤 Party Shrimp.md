# 🍤 Party Shrimp   
# ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️  
- 1 Tbsp olive oil  
- 3 tsp honey  
- 1 tsp lemon juice  
- 1 garlic clove  minced  
- 1/2 tsp paprika  
- 1/2 tsp italian seasoning  
- 1/2 tsp dried basil  
- 1/4 tsp black pepper  
- 1 lbs uncooked shrimp, peeled and deveined  
- 2 Tbsp coconut flour  
- 2 cups cooked rice  
  
1. In a bowl or shallow dish, combine the first eight ingredients. Add shrimp; toss to coat. Refrigerate 2 hours.  
2. Cook shrimp in the marinade until fully cooked.  
3. Remove shrimp and set aside.  
4. Add coconut flour to the marinade.  
5. Cook on high whisking vigorously to prevent burning or sticking.  
6. Cook until sauce begins to thicken about 8 minutes.  
7. Place 6 to 8 shrimp on a bed of rice and drizzle the sauce over the rice and loop shrimp.
