- 3 egg whites  
- 200g sunflower seeds  
- 200g pumpkin seeds  
- 1/2 cup desiccated coconut  
- 1 Tbsp vanilla extract  
- 1 tsp cinnamon  
- zest of 1 orange (optional)  
- 2 Tbsp collagen

1. Soak the dates in hot water for a min of 10min, then strain off the water.  
2. Preheat the oven to 170ºC.  
3. Add all of the ingredients to a food processor.  
4. Blitz for 1 min, scrape down the side and blitz again for 1 min.  
5. Press the batter into a lined 10x8 inch baking tin or even better a silicon tray  
6. Bake for 30-40min, they are ready when the entire surface is a toasted golden colour.  
7. Remove from the oven and immediately turn off the oven.  
8. Carefully flip out the bake upside down onto an oven rack and slice into bars.  
9. Separate the bars slightly giving them a little space between each other.  
10. Return the bars back to the oven for a further 10min to cook the underside.  
11. Remove then leave to cool and crisp.
