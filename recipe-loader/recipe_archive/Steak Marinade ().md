# Steak Marinade (?)  
- 1/3 cup soy sauce or coconut aminos  
- 1/2 cup olive oil   
- 1/3 cup lemon juice   
- 1/4 Worcestershire sauce  
- 1 1/2 Tbsp. garlic powder  
- 3 Tbsp. dried basil  
- 1 1/2 Tbsp. dried parsley flakes  
- 1 tsp. ground white pepper  
- 1/4 tsp. hot pepper sauce (optional)  
  
1. Blend all ingredients on high for 30 seconds until thoroughly mixed  
2. Pour marinade over steak and refrigerate for no more than 8 hours; 4 hours is best.  
3. cook steak to desired temp
