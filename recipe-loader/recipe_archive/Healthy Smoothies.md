# Healthy Smoothies   
  
## Spinach & Avocado  
- 1  Avocado  
- 1  Cup Spinach   
- 1  Large Banana  
- 1  Cup of Milk  
- 1  Handful of Ice  
  
## Banana Cream Pie ⭐️⭐️⭐️  
- 1 Large Banana Frozen  
- 1 Cup Greek Yogurt   
- 1/2 Teaspoon Vanilla Extract  
- 2 Tablespoons Honey  
- 1/3 Cup Milk  
- 1 Handful Ice Cubes   
  
## Chocolate Chia Cherry ⭐️⭐️⭐️  
- 1 Cup Milk  
- 2 Tablespoons Chia  
- 1 tablespoon Unsweetened Cacoa Powder  
- 2 Cups Pitted Cherries   
- 1 Ripe Banana  
- 1 Hadful Ice Cubes   
  
## Mixed Berry Cobbler   
- 1/2 Cup Milk  
- 1/2 Cup Blueberries   
- 1/2 Cup Strawberries   
- 1/2 Cup Blackberries   
- 2-3 Dates  
  
## Peach Milkshake ⭐️⭐️⭐️  
- 1 Cup Milk  
- 1 Cup of Peaches   
- 1/2 Cup Pineapple Juice  
- 1/2 Frozen Banana   
- 1 Teaspoon Coconut Oil  
  
## Orange Creamcicle  
- 1/2 cup yogurt  
- 1/2 cup milk  
- 4 Tbsp. frozen orange juice  
- 6-8 ice cubes   
  
## Chocolate Peanut Butter & Banana  
- 16 oz. milk  
- 2 scoops chocolate protein powder   
- 1 banana  
- 2 Tbs. peanut butter  
- 2 Tbs. Flaxseed  
  
# Chai Smoothie
