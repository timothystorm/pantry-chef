# 🥤Gatorade - Homemade   
* 20 oz water  
* 1/2 tsp. sea salt  
* 1/4 cup honey   
* 1/4 juice or tea  
  
## Juice options  
- lemon & lime  
- orange juice   
- mojito - let 6 mint leaves infuse in water for an hour  
- blueberry & pomegranate - let handful of blueberries infuse in water for an hour, then add 2 Tbsp of pomegranate juice  
- Cherry juice - not concentrate  
  
## Storage  
To store homemade Gatorade, pour it into a clean, airtight container and refrigerate it immediately. Label the container with the date you made it and drink the Gatorade within 3-5 days for optimal freshness and safety.
