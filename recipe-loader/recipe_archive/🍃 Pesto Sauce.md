# 🍃 Pesto Sauce   
# ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐  
  
- 2 cups fresh basil leaves (Packed)  
- 1/4 cup toasted pine nuts (Lightly)  
- 2 large garlic cloves  
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan (or Romano Cheese)  
- 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil  
  
1. Place all the ingredients in a food processor except the oil, and pulse.  
  
2. Start to add in the oil slowly, pulsing continuously until you have a smooth paste.  
  
3. Use immediately to top any pasta dish. (If you do not plan to use it right away, store in a container with an additional layer of olive oil on top to prevent discoloration.)
