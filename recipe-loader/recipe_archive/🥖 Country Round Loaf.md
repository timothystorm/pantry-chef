# 🥖 Country Round Loaf   
# ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️  
- 560 g 00 flour  
- 190 g whole wheat flour  
- 600 g 100”-110” water  
- 17 g salt  
- 3 g yeast  
  
## Half loaf  
- 280 g 00 flour  
- 95 g while wheat flour  
- 300 g 100”-110” water (400g)  
- 9 g salt  
- 2 g yeast  
  
1. Mix flours and water together by hand till there are no dry spots  
2. Cover and let rest for 10 minutes  
3. Mix in salt then yeast folding dough over itself for 4-5 minutes  
4. Cover and let rest for 15-20 minutes  
5. Fold and stretch dough over itself for another 2 minute   
6. Cover and let rest for 45-60 min.  
  
Rise #1  
1. Do not degass  
2. Fold and stretch dough over itself for another 2 minutes - only pull till you feel resistance.  
3. Cover and let rest for 90-120 (180-240) min. or until it’s tripled in size  
  
Rise #2  
1. Do not degass  
2. On a floured surface fold the dough over itself for a few times till you have a nice round; cup your hands underneath the dough to form a ball - only pull till you feel resistance  
3. Place in a proving bowl for for 45-60 (90-120) min  
  
Rise #3  
1. 30 min. before proving is complete add a dutch oven to a 475’ stove.  
2. Tip the dough over into the dutch oven, put the lid on and cook for 35 min.
