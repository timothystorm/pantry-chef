# Pumpkin Soup ⬜️⬜️⬜️⬜️⬜️  
- 6 cups chicken stock  
- 1 1/2 tsp salt  
- 4 cups pumpkin purée   
- 1 cup chopped onion or 1/2 cup chopped shallots  
- 1/2 tsp fresh chopped thyme  
- 1 garlic clove minced  
- 1/2 cup heavy whipping cream  
- 5 whole pepper corns  
  
1. Heat stock, salt, pumpkin, onion, thyme, garlic, and peppercorns. Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low, and simmer for 30 minutes uncovered.  
2. Reduce heat and purée with an immersion blender.  
3. Reduce heat and purée with an immersion blender.  
4. Bring to a boil again. Reduce heat to low, and simmer for another 30 minutes, uncovered. Stir in heavy cream. Pour into soup bowls.  
5. Bring to a boil again. Reduce heat to low, and simmer for another 30 minutes, uncovered. Stir in heavy cream. Pour into soup bowls.
