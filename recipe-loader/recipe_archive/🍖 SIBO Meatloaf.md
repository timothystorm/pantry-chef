# ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️  
- 1 lbs. ground beef  
- 1 lbs. ground turkey   
- 1 lbs. bacon, finely chopped   
- 2 eggs  
- 2 Tbsp. Low FODMAP ketchup   
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan   
- 1/2 tsp. thyme   
- 1/2 tsp. marjoram   
- 1/2 tsp. basil  
- 1/2 tsp. kosher salt  
- 1/2 tsp. black pepper  
  
Preheat oven to 375° F  
  
1. add all ingredients to a large bill and mix thoroughly   
2. place in a greased bread pan  
3. cook for 45-60 minutes  
4. allow to rest for 15-20 minutes before serving
