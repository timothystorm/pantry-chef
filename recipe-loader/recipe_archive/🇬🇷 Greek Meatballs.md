## Tzatziki   
- 2 cups plain greek yogurt  
- 1 persian cucumber  
- 1 garlic clove  
- 3 tablespoons dill  
- 3 tablespoons mint  
- 2 teaspoons red wine vinegar  
  
Blend all ingredients and flavor with salt to taste.  
  
## Greek Meatballs  
Mix 1 pound ground lamb, 1 egg and 1/2 cup each breadcrumbs and tzatziki with your hands. Form into 1-inch balls. Bake on a rimmed baking sheet at 425 degrees F until browned, about 15 minutes. Serve with the remaining tzatziki; top with herbs. (Makes about 24.)
