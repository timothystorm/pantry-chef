# Anti Inflammatory Porridge ⬜️⬜️⬜️⬜️⬜️  
- ¼ cup walnuts, chopped and toasted  
- 2 tablespoons hemp seeds, toasted  
- 2 tablespoons chia seeds   
- 1 cup almond milk, unsweetened   
- ¼ cup coconut milk, unsweetened   
- ¼ cup coconut, shredded and toasted   
- ¼ cup almond butter   
- 1 tablespoon coconut oil, melted   
- ½ teaspoon turmeric powder   
- 1 teaspoon bee pollen   
- A pinch of black pepper  
  
Heat up a pot with the almond and coconut milk over medium heat, add the walnuts, hemp seeds, chia seeds, coconut, turmeric, black pepper and the bee pollen, stir, cook for 5 minutes. Take off heat, add the coconut oil and the almond butter, stir and let sit for 10 minutes then divide into 2 bowls and serve.
