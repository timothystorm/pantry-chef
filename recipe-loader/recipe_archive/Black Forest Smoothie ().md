- ¼ avocado  
- 2 tbs chia seeds  
- 2 handfuls baby spinach leaves  
- 4 heaped tsp cocoa powder  
- 2 tbs maple syrup  
- handful of ice  
- 4 cherries, pitted and chopped  
- 1 oz dark chocolate, grated (optional)
