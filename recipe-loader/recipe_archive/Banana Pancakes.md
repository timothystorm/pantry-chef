# Banana Pancakes  
- 50g oats  
- 1 banana  
- 4 tbsp plain yoghurt  
- 1 Tbsp. coconut oil   
- 1 Tbsp. coconut oil   
- 1 Tbsp. coconut oil   
  
* Mill porridge oats in a blender to create a flour consistency  
* Mash banana until a smooth paste  
* Mash banana until a smooth paste  
* Mix oats, banana and yoghurt together in a bowl  
* Add water to thin the mixture if necessary  
* Fry in oil until golden  
* Serve with more yoghurt and a drizzle of maple syrup
