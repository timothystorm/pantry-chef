# Masala Curry (?)  
- 1 pound of skinless and boneless turkey breasts, cut into 2” slices   
- 1 (14.5 oz.) can of diced tomatoes   
- 1 tsp curry powder   
- 1 tsp garam masala   
- 1 tsp salt   
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder  
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder  
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder  
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder  
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder  
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder  
- 1/4 tsp dried cilantro   
- 1/2 cup dairy-free sour cream/yogurt (optional)  
  
1. Add all the ingredients (saving sour cream) into a food processor.   
2. Grind it down until fully blended.   
3. Taste to see if you want to make any alterations.   
4. Add your spice mixture to the slow cooker. Add your turkey slices. Cover.  
5. Cook on Low for 1h 45 until chicken is cooked through.   
6. Add the cream or yoghurt and stir.   
7. Serve with Basmati or Pilau rice, whichever you prefer.
